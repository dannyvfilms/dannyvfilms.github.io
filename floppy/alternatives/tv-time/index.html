A clear boundary

The comments were part of it.

!

If comments were the best part, Floppy is not that replacement.

Episode theories, jokes, reactions, and the feeling of watching together were real. Floppy does not recreate that social network. It is for the personal record underneath: what you watched, what comes next, and what you want to keep. See the full feature guide →

Bring your history with you

You already lost the service.Keep the history.

TV Time’s service has closed, but your saved GDPR archive or tracking CSV gives the watch history a way forward. Floppy can import the files, map the episodes into your library, and keep the result reviewable.

Import supported

Bring the archive you saved.

Upload a gdpr-data.zip or tracking-prod-records-v2.csv. Floppy imports watched episodes and progress, keeps original dates where available, and surfaces records that need a closer look.

Keep the original file: it remains your source copy while you verify the imported history.

After the import

Keep the next copy yourself.

Review what matched, keep the details that matter, then use Floppy’s portable exports and scheduled backups so this history has a durable home.
