A clear fit

A replacement does not have to be a replica.

If you used Trakt as a database and tracking tool, Floppy is built around that same continuity, with a different home for the data.

Floppy is for you if…

  • Your watch history has become too valuable to leave in one hosted account.
  • You want progress, lists, collection, calendar, and integrations in one place.
  • You would rather own a portable copy than hope your workflow survives the next product change.

Know what is different.

Floppy is a personal, self-hosted media library, not a hosted social network or a promise to reproduce every Trakt screen. The trade is straightforward: more control over your data, with a product that keeps growing around your own media life.
