The Floppy integrations guide

Bring what you already use.

Floppy does not ask you to abandon Plex, Steam, Last.fm, or the rest of your media stack. Connect the services that already hold pieces of your media life, then bring the activity and history together in one self-hosted home.

⇄

One library, many starting points.

Some connections update as you watch or listen. Others check in on a schedule, or give you a simple way to bring over an export. The useful part is the same: your history can stay connected to the tools you already use.

Live

As it happens

Playback events and scrobbles can appear while you watch, listen, or play.

Scheduled

Checks in for you

Floppy periodically pulls new activity or collection changes from the service.

One-time import

Bring over a snapshot

Export a file or connect an account once to backfill the history you already have.

Movies & TV

Your watch history, wherever it starts.

Connect a personal media server, a request tool, a library automation service, or a tracking site. Floppy keeps the distinction between watching something, owning it, requesting it, and simply wanting to remember it.

Plex

LiveImport

Personal media server

One-click Plex sign-in connects your server without typing a password into Floppy. Live playback can show on your home screen, mark titles watched, import existing history, and mirror your Watchlist.

Good to know: Plex Pass is required for live sync; history import works for everyone.

Jellyfin

LiveImport

Free, open-source media server

Connect with your server address and an API key. Floppy syncs playback in real time, can push watched status back to Jellyfin, and can backfill built-in playback history.

Connection: two-way watched-status sync keeps both sides aligned.

Emby

Live

Self-hosted media server

Connect with a per-server webhook link to sync movies and TV as you watch them.

Good to know: Emby music is not supported.

Kodi

Live

Open-source media player

A free community add-on sends your movie and TV playback activity to Floppy while you use Kodi on a TV box, computer, or other device.

Connection: install the community add-on, then let it report playback.

Stremio

LiveScheduled

Media-center app

Sign in with Stremio. Floppy marks titles in progress as soon as you press play and syncs finished history automatically every couple of hours.

Connection: account sign-in; progress is immediate, finished history catches up periodically.

Seerr

Live

Overseerr / Jellyseerr request tool

Connect a webhook link and add requested or approved titles to a Floppy list automatically, useful for an “on the way” shelf.

Good to know: requests are tracked, not watched; shared households are supported.

Radarr & Sonarr

Scheduled

Library automation tools

Connect with a server address and API key. Floppy checks in every couple of hours to track which movies and shows you actually own.

Good to know: this is collection ownership, not watch history.

Trakt

Import

Community watch tracker

Use a public username or sign in for private profile access. Floppy imports watch history, ratings, watchlists, and custom lists.

Alternative: upload an offline Trakt export file directly.

MDBList

SyncImport

Curated movie and TV lists

Connect with an MDBList API key to import watch history, watchlist, ratings, and lists, with custom lists kept in sync going forward.

Connection: API key from your MDBList account.

IMDb

One-time import

Movie and TV ratings database

Export your IMDb ratings and upload the file to bring years of ratings into Floppy in one quick pass.

Connection: file upload; no ongoing account connection required.

Anime & Manga

Bring your lists along.

Move your anime and manga tracking history into the same library as the rest of your media, whether your list lives on a community site or in a broader watch tracker.

MyAnimeList

One-time import

Anime and manga tracking community

Import your list with a public username.

Good to know: set your MyAnimeList list to public first.

AniList

Import

Modern anime and manga tracker

Import with a public username, or sign in when you need private-list access.

Connection: public username or account sign-in.

Kitsu

One-time import

Anime and manga community

Import your list with your public Kitsu user ID.

Connection: public user ID.

Simkl

Import

Watch tracker for TV, movies, and anime

Sign in to import your history, then choose whether Simkl anime belongs in Floppy’s Anime section or with regular TV and movies.

Good to know: choose the destination that matches how you browse.

Music

Keep the songs moving.

Floppy can pull from a music history service or accept listens directly from any scrobbler that speaks the ListenBrainz protocol.

Last.fm

ScheduledImport

Music scrobbling service

Connect with a public username to pull new plays regularly, then optionally backfill your whole listening history.

Good to know: your Last.fm scrobbles need to be public.

Koito

Scheduled

Self-hosted Last.fm alternative

Connect your Koito server with an API key. Floppy imports the full history on first connect, then checks every 15 minutes for new plays.

Good to know: Floppy only reads from Koito; Koito remains the source of truth.

ListenBrainz-compatible scrobblers

Live

Direct music activity

Multi-Scrobbler, Navidrome, Feishin, Symfonium, Pano Scrobbler, and other compatible tools can send listens straight to Floppy in real time.

Connection: point the scrobbler at Floppy with your API token, with no import needed.

Podcasts

Remember every episode.

Sync the podcast apps and servers you already use so podcast listening sits alongside movies, books, music, and games. Audiobookshelf also carries audiobook progress.

Pocket Casts

Scheduled

Podcast app

Sign in with your Pocket Casts account. Floppy imports your listening history and checks every couple of hours for new activity.

Connection: account sign-in.

GPodder

Scheduled

Open podcast sync standard

Connect with your server address and login to pull listening activity from GPodder, AntennaPod, or a compatible self-hosted sync server.

Connection: server address and account login.

Audiobookshelf

Scheduled

Self-hosted audiobook and podcast server

Connect with your server address and an API token. Floppy regularly syncs listening and reading progress and can fill in missing details automatically.

Scope: podcasts and audiobooks.

▤

Books

Make the shelf portable.

Bring reading history, audiobook progress, and book metadata into the same personal library. Export-based connections are intentionally simple: your data starts as a file you can keep.

Audiobookshelf

Scheduled

Self-hosted audiobook and podcast server

Use the same server address and API token connection to sync audiobook reading progress into Floppy.

See also: podcast listening is supported through the same integration.

Storyteller

Scheduled

Self-hosted audiobook and ebook server

Approve a code on your Storyteller server, with no password to type into Floppy. Floppy checks in every couple of hours.

Progress: books are marked read after enough progress is recorded.

Goodreads

One-time import

Book-tracking and social reading site

Export your Goodreads library and upload it to bring your reading history into Floppy.

Connection: file upload; your export remains yours.

The StoryGraph

One-time import

Reading tracker focused on stats and moods

Export your StoryGraph data and upload it to bring your history into Floppy.

Connection: file upload.

Hardcover

One-time import

Modern book-tracking community

Export and upload your Hardcover data. An API key is optional when you want richer book details.

Connection: file upload, with optional API key.

⌁

Games

Track the backlog you actually play.

Game integrations focus on play history, hours, and backlog context, the parts of a gaming life that are easiest to lose across storefronts and consoles.

Steam

Sync

PC gaming storefront

Connect with your public Steam ID. Floppy tracks what you have played, how long you played, and adjusts planning, playing, or on-hold status from actual playtime.

Connection: public Steam ID.

Xbox

Import

Microsoft gaming ecosystem

Connect with a free key from a companion sign-in service to import played games and hours logged.

Good to know: played games only; not your full library, and hours are not available for every game.

PlayStation Network

Import

Sony gaming ecosystem

Paste a token generated from your PlayStation account to import played games and logged hours.

Good to know: the token needs refreshing every couple of months; this is played history, not the full owned library.

HowLongToBeat

One-time import

Game-length and backlog tracker

Upload your export file to bring your HowLongToBeat history into Floppy.

Connection: file upload.

Grouvee

One-time import

Board game and video game collection tracker

Upload your Grouvee export to bring your collection into Floppy.

Connection: file upload.

Bringing everything from elsewhere

No lock-in, no lost history.

A portable library needs an exit as well as an entrance. Floppy’s own backup format can restore your library, and Yamtrack exports can move over in one step.

Floppy backup format

Import / export

Portable Floppy library file

Export your whole Floppy library to a file, then bring it back later for a backup or when moving between installs.

Why it matters: your history stays portable.

Yamtrack

One-time import

Floppy’s starting point

If you are coming from Yamtrack, upload your export and move everything across in one step.

Connection: export file; Floppy began as a Yamtrack fork.

Built for your stack

A good fit for the home server crowd.

Jellyfin, Koito, GPodder, Audiobookshelf, Storyteller, Radarr, and Sonarr are not afterthoughts here. Floppy is designed to sit beside the self-hosted services you already run, while still making room for public trackers and simple exports.

Connect what is useful today, export when you need to, and keep one history that is not locked inside a single service.
