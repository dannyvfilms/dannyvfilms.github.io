What Plex unlocks

More than a watched checkbox.

Plex can be a source for activity, ownership, planning, and metadata at the same time. Floppy keeps those jobs distinct so one connection can support the way your own media setup actually works.

01 / History

Bring over what you already watched.

Import movies, shows, episodes, anime, music tracks, and audiobooks. Run a one-time backfill or schedule recurring imports, with options for all libraries or a selected set.

02 / Live activity

See playback while it happens.

Plex webhooks can update play, pause, resume, stop, scrobble, and rating activity. Current playback can appear on Floppy Home, with history imports available to reconcile anything missed.

03 / Planning

Keep your Watchlist useful.

Floppy can mirror Plex movies and shows into Planning every 15 minutes. Entries you have changed in Floppy stay yours instead of being overwritten by the sync.

04 / Music + books

Let a Plex Music library be more than music.

Choose Auto-detect, Music, or Audiobooks for each library. Chapter activity can become book progress, while regular tracks flow through Floppy’s music history.

05 / Collection

Remember what is in the library.

Capture resolution, HDR, audio codec, channels, bitrate, and container details so your Plex collection is useful even when you are not currently watching something.

06 / Household

Share selected activity safely.

Share selected Plex usernames or libraries with another Floppy user without handing over your Plex password, account token, or server credentials.

A sensible first run

Connect once, then tune the details.

The first import gives Floppy context. The choices after that determine which parts of Plex stay in sync and how much of your server stack you want represented.

1

Connect Plex

Use the account sign-in flow from Floppy’s Import page.

2

Choose libraries

Sync all accessible libraries or select specific servers and sections.

3

Run history import

Backfill the activity you already have, safely deduplicated on repeat runs.

4

Enable webhooks

Add the Floppy endpoint in Plex for near-real-time playback updates.

5

Review matches

Correct an unresolved item once and reuse that decision later.

One important import setting:

Overwrite mode is powerful, but it can affect TMDB-backed records created by another source when they share the same identity. For most first imports, a normal non-overwrite run is the safer place to start.

◉

Live webhooks

Fast updates, with a repair path.

Webhooks let Plex tell Floppy what is happening without waiting for the next scheduled import. They are intentionally inbound: Plex remains the playback source, and Floppy reflects that activity in its own history and progress surfaces.

Play / resume / pauseUpdate live playback state, including what is playing and how far through it you are.
StopUpdates or clears resume state; stopping alone is not treated as a completed watch.
ScrobbleRecords the completed watch and updates Floppy history.
RateCan sync movie and TV ratings, though Plex rating events are less dependable than library scans.

What setup requires

Plex webhooks require Plex Pass, webhooks enabled in your Plex Server settings, and an endpoint added through app.plex.tv.

  • Your Floppy instance needs to be reachable by the Plex server.
  • Rotating the Floppy webhook token means updating the URL in Plex.
  • The exact endpoint is shown in Floppy’s Integrations settings.

Why imports still matter

A webhook is a great live signal, not a complete archive. Historical imports are the reconciliation layer when a server misses an event, a connection is briefly unavailable, or you connect a library after the fact.

  • Repeated imports are normally deduplicated.
  • Extras, trailers, and clips are ignored.
  • Partial progress does not become a completed watch during history import.

Plex Music libraries

One library type can hold two kinds of listening.

Plex stores audiobooks inside Music libraries often enough that Floppy gives the choice its own control. Automatic detection looks for audiobook-shaped signals, and you can override it when you know your library better.

Music

Track plays through Floppy’s music pipeline, with album and artist relationships, playback history, and collection details. MusicBrainz identifiers are preferred when they are available.

Audiobooks

Route a library to books and Floppy creates one book per audiobook album. Chapter-level activity is combined into progress, with author, summary, genres, runtime, and cover art carried into the book record.

Book tracking must be enabled for audiobook records to be created.

✓

Control and recovery

Useful when Plex metadata is imperfect.

Matching is a normal part of bringing a personal library into a broader media database. Floppy gives you a way to correct the exception without changing Plex itself.

Floppy reads from Plex

Floppy can use Plex identity, library, history, webhook, Watchlist, playback, artwork, and media-format data to build its own records and views.

Floppy does not rewrite Plex

Watched state, ratings, and collection changes are not pushed back into Plex. Watchlist sync flows from Plex into Floppy Planning, not the other direction.

Fix a match once

Matching Review lets you choose the right movie, show, episode, anime, music, or book record. Saved decisions are reused by later imports and webhooks.

Keep sharing narrow

Webhook sharing is filtered by Plex username and library. The recipient must enable the share, and the owner can revoke it without sharing account credentials.
